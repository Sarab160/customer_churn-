customerID	Unique ID for each customer
gender	Customer’s gender (Male/Female)
SeniorCitizen	Whether the customer is a senior (1 = Yes, 0 = No)
Partner	Does the customer have a partner? (Yes/No)
Dependents	Does the customer have dependents (kids or others)? (Yes/No)
tenure	How many months the customer has stayed with the company
PhoneService	Does the customer have phone service? (Yes/No)
MultipleLines	Does the customer have multiple phone lines?
InternetService	Type of internet service (DSL, Fiber optic, or None)
OnlineSecurity	Does the customer have online security service?
OnlineBackup	Does the customer use online backup service?
DeviceProtection	Does the customer use device protection service?
TechSupport	Does the customer have tech support service?
StreamingTV	Does the customer stream TV using the service?
StreamingMovies	Does the customer stream movies using the service?
Contract	Type of contract (Month-to-month, One year, Two year)
PaperlessBilling	Does the customer use paperless billing? (Yes/No)
PaymentMethod	How the customer pays (e.g., electronic check, credit card)
MonthlyCharges	How much the customer is charged per month
TotalCharges	Total amount the customer has been charged
Churn	Target column – Did the customer leave? (Yes/No)
